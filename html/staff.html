Dr. Isla Raman

Archaeologist

Specializes in ritual tools and mountain sanctuaries. Holder of 3 Fulbright fellowships, Dr. Raman has spent over 15 years excavating sacred sites across the Himalayas and Andes.

Her groundbreaking work on pre-Columbian ceremonial practices has reshaped our understanding of ancient spiritual traditions.

Dr. Raj Malik

Anthropologist

Studies burial customs and symbolic storytelling in oral traditions. Dr. Malik's extensive fieldwork spans three continents, documenting disappearing cultural practices.

His comparative research on death rituals has been published in over 40 peer-reviewed journals and has contributed significantly to our understanding of human belief systems.
